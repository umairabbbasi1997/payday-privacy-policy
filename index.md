# Privacy Policy – Payday by ZUNO

**Effective date:** November 6, 2026  
**Last updated:** November 6, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to Payday, a personal expense and budget management application developed and published under the ZUNO brand.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Payday accesses, stores, uses, and protects your information when you use our application.

By using Payday, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

## 2. Information We Handle

Payday is designed to help you manage your personal finances. Depending on the features you use, the application may store the following information:

- **Financial information:** Income, expenses, transaction descriptions, account names, budgets, bills, and financial records you enter.
- **Preferences:** App settings, language preferences, theme selection, and other customization options.
- **App security information:** Information required to operate app-lock and security features.
- **Backup information:** Backup files and associated information when you choose to use backup and restore features.

The financial information you enter is intended for your personal expense-management purposes.

## 3. How Your Information Is Stored

Payday is designed with local-first data storage.

Your financial records are stored locally on your device. ZUNO does not operate a central financial database for storing your personal transactions.

If you uninstall the application or clear its data, locally stored information may be permanently lost unless you have created a backup.

## 4. Google Drive Backup and Restore

If you choose to use Google Drive backup and restore:

- Payday may request permission to access the Google Drive functionality required for its backup feature.
- Your backup file is stored in your own Google Drive account.
- Google processes your Google account information and Drive data under its own privacy policies.
- ZUNO does not use your personal financial backup for advertising or sell it to third parties.
- You can manage or delete your backup files through your Google Drive account.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google Drive backups are optional. You can use the application's local features without enabling cloud backup, where supported.

## 5. How We Use Information

Information handled by Payday is used to:

- Record and organize income and expenses.
- Calculate budgets and spending summaries.
- Help you monitor bills and financial activity.
- Provide reports and financial overviews.
- Support app preferences and security features.
- Create and restore backups when requested.

We do not sell your personal financial information.

## 6. Information Sharing

ZUNO does not sell, rent, or trade your personal financial records.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers only where necessary to provide a feature you choose to use, such as Google Drive backup.

We do not share your financial records with advertisers for targeted advertising.

## 7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by Payday.

Depending on the feature and device, these measures may include:

- Local device storage.
- Application security and app-lock functionality.
- Secure handling of authentication information.
- Protection of backup data.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security. Users should keep their devices and Google accounts protected.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

Financial information stored locally remains on your device until you delete it, clear the application data, or uninstall the application.

Backup files stored in Google Drive remain subject to your Google Drive account settings and retention choices. You can delete these files directly from Google Drive.

If you have questions or need assistance regarding information handled by Payday, contact us using the details below.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Payday is intended for a general audience and is not specifically designed for children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a ZUNO-operated online account service.

## 10. Third-Party Services

Payday may use third-party services to provide specific application functionality.

These services operate under their own privacy policies. Users should review the relevant policies when enabling third-party features.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
